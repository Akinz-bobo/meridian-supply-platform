## Context

The situation that forces a decision: the problem, the constraints and what makes it hard. Facts, not the answer.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to live with. Include the costs.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was seriously weighed, and the reason it was not chosen.
